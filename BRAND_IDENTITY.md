## Canonical visual source — do not reinterpret

The owner-approved image supplied in this project is the single source of truth for the Otya master logo and visual identity. It must be used exactly as shown.

Preserve the supplied artwork's:
- Orbital O symbol, sweep/orbit and floating square motif.
- Original Otya wordmark lettering, weight, proportions and spacing.
- Navy, blue, cyan and turquoise gradients.
- Exact tagline: **One ecosystem. Endless possibilities.**
- Exact brand line: **Innovate | Connect | Grow**
- Exact developer credit in the artwork: **Developed by OtyaHQ**
- Product icon artwork, colours and layout as shown.
- Corporate footer artwork and wording as shown.

**Forbidden:** redrawing the symbol, substituting a font for the wordmark, inventing SVG approximations, changing gradients, recolouring, altering proportions, or treating the full banner as a substitute for each separate product icon.

Only the supplied original image and faithful direct pixel crops from it are approved. If a required production format cannot be extracted faithfully, keep the original image rather than inventing a replacement.

Where product marks are required, use the exact icon artwork from the approved source image where the product is represented there, while retaining the current product name and product boundaries above.

## Asset rules

1. Retain the complete original image as the reference master.
2. Create separate assets only by exact cropping from the original image.
3. Keep original pixels, lettering, gradients and proportions. Do not redraw.
4. Use full artwork for company/brand presentations and direct crops for appropriate compact placements.
5. Never use a low-resolution preview as the production master.
6. Do not replace app icons, favicons, websites or emails until the correct source-derived assets are uploaded and verified.
7. Keep technical identifiers stable unless a reviewed migration explicitly changes them.

## Corporate and legal accuracy

Use **Otya Holdings Limited** as the parent-company wording in the approved visual identity, but do not claim incorporation, trademark registration, certification, product release or payment-service approval unless independently verified in the relevant official records.
