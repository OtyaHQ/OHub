# Otya Brand Identity Standard

**Status:** Approved master brand direction  
**Brand owner:** Otya Holdings Limited (corporate name as directed by the owner)  
**Developer identity:** OtyaHQ  
**Umbrella brand:** Otya

This document records the approved Otya visual direction. The uploaded master artwork is the visual reference; production assets must be derived from the original artwork and prepared as purpose-built files, not by using the full banner as every logo or app icon.

## Master identity

- Primary wordmark: Otya
- Primary symbol: the blue/cyan orbital O mark with the connected square motif
- Brand promise: **One ecosystem. Endless possibilities.**
- Brand line: **Innovate | Connect | Grow**
- Corporate descriptor: **Otya Holdings Limited**
- Corporate tagline: **Powering a smarter, more connected world.**
- Developer credit: **Developed by OtyaHQ**

## Visual system

- Core palette: deep navy, royal blue, bright cyan, and white.
- Use the orbital symbol and wordmark together for the master lockup.
- Use the symbol alone for favicons, app icons, compact navigation, and avatars.
- Maintain strong contrast, clear space, and legibility at small sizes.
- Do not redraw, stretch, skew, recolour arbitrarily, or add effects to the approved master mark.
- Create light-background and dark-background exports from the approved master artwork.
- Use editable vector artwork for scalable production wherever a faithful vector master can be prepared; retain the supplied raster artwork as the source reference.

## Product identity

The master Otya identity unifies the family. Each product keeps a clear product name and may use a restrained product accent/icon while retaining the master brand system.

- **Otya OS** — unified platform and shared experience.
- **Opoint** — retail, business operations and financial services application.
- **Omy Play** — local-first media application.
- **OEnd** — shared backend and integration layer; internal service identity.
- **OID** — shared account identity and security service.
- **OHub** — public developer and partner documentation.
- **O** — private owner intelligence application.

Use the product names above in current product interfaces and technical documentation. The names shown in the supplied banner are a concept-era family presentation and must not silently override the current product naming and boundaries.

## Correct use

- Public umbrella presentation: **Otya**.
- Corporate/legal presentation: **Otya Holdings Limited**, subject to the company's actual registration status and approved legal records.
- Developer attribution: **OtyaHQ**.
- Product attribution: **[Product] by Otya** where appropriate.
- Never imply that a product, payment service, certification, trademark registration, or company incorporation is complete unless verified.
- Never put credentials, internal endpoints, customer data, or private infrastructure details in brand assets.

## Asset family to maintain

1. Master horizontal wordmark.
2. Standalone orbital symbol.
3. Compact square app icon.
4. Light and dark logo variants.
5. Product lockups/icons for Opoint, Omy Play, Otya OS, OHub and OID.
6. Corporate banner and social/profile artwork.
7. Favicon and monochrome variants.

All derivatives must preserve the approved symbol, proportions, palette, and visual character. Do not use the entire banner as a substitute for individual product logos.
