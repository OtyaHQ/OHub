# Otya Security Policy

Security reports should be handled privately.

## Report a vulnerability

Email **security@otya.site** with a clear subject such as `Otya security report`.

Please include:

- the affected Otya product, app version, or public endpoint;
- a concise description of the issue;
- safe reproduction steps;
- expected and observed behavior;
- the impact you believe is possible;
- any non-sensitive supporting evidence.

## Please do not

- publish credentials, tokens, private keys, OTPs, personal data, or exploit details in a public issue;
- access or modify data that is not yours;
- disrupt production services or intentionally exhaust resources;
- use social engineering against users or staff;
- publicly disclose an unresolved vulnerability before a reasonable remediation period.

## Scope

The official Otya website, Otya Media, OtyaPoint public surfaces, Otya account services and connected Otya services are in scope where Otya operates or controls the affected service. Third-party services are governed by their own security programs.

Otya's current operations team will acknowledge good-faith reports and prioritize remediation based on demonstrated risk. Otya Holdings Limited is the intended parent entity and its URSB registration is in progress; this policy does not claim that the company is already incorporated.
